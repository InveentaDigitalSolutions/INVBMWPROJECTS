How to read this dictionary

Weekly Plans is the fact table — one row per planned rail transport. Everything else is a dimension or config table you relate to it. Each transport moves through a status lifecycle (00 → 42), and many columns come in stage families (Required → Requested → Ordered → Confirmed → Delivered → Loaded → Delivered at destination) — the Weekly Plans section is grouped that way.

measure

Numeric / currency column — a natural SUM/AVG target. Wagon counts and costs are additive.

key

Lookup (relationship). Model as a link to the named dimension; don't aggregate it.

choice

Option set / Yes-No. Power BI reads the integer option value, not the label — use the value map shown under the field.

date

Date column — relate to Calendars for a time axis. See the date-storage note below.

Two gotchas for reporting:  ① State filter — most tables have statecode (0 = Aktiv, 1 = Inaktiv). Filter to Aktiv for live figures.  ② DateOnly storage — date columns are stored in UTC; a date the app wrote as local midnight lands as 22:00Z the day before. Read the date part directly (don't apply a timezone shift in the model) to avoid an off-by-one day.

Model & key relationships

Suggested star schema: Weekly Plans (fact) in the centre, linked out to the dimensions below. Calendars is the date dimension — connect it to Transport Date (and optionally Order Date). The six Responsible for … lookups all point at Shareholders, so you'll need role-playing dimensions (duplicate/alias Shareholders per role) if you report on more than one at once.

Setup & operations Bedienungsanleitung

RTS is not a single app — it is a canvas app for BMW staff, a set of 27 cloud flows that move transports along on a schedule and send the e-mails, and four web forms (masks) that external partners fill in without needing a licence. The three parts talk to each other through one Dataverse table, Weekly Plans, and through the transport status. Understand that and the rest follows.

① Set up

Fill the master data in dependency order (§ Master data setup). Done once per plant / lane, then extended as the network grows.

② Operate

Per calendar week: generate the week from the Musterwoche, then let the status chain run. Staff only intervene where a decision is needed (§ Running the system).

③ Maintain

Annual: extend Calendars and the holiday flags. Ongoing: people (Shareholders), lanes (Routes), deadlines, reasons.

④ Own

Environment variables, connection references and the externally-hosted masks all have to be transferred to customer accounts (§ Environment & handover).

Who does what

Every person in the process is a row in Shareholders. The multi-select rts_role on that row decides both what they see in the app and which e-mails they receive — it is the single most important field for day-to-day operation.

Center of Competence 593090003

BMW process owner. Full app access: checks demands and orders, plans the week, runs dunning and cancellation. Owns the master data.

Factory Dispatch Point 593090002

The Quelle. Confirms empty-wagon arrival and loading — in the app, or by mask if the site has no RTS access.

Supplier 593090001

The rail service provider. No app access — confirms or edits orders only through the order-confirmation mask linked in the e-mail.

Destination 593090000

The Senke / receiving point. No app access — confirms arrival of the loaded transport through the Empfangsort mask.

!A person with no rts_email, or with statecode = Inaktiv, silently receives nothing. This is by far the most common cause of "the notification never arrived" — the flows filter recipients on exactly those two fields.

Master data setup — in this order

Each step depends on the ones above it, so work top to bottom. Fields marked must are required for the system to function — not merely required by Dataverse. Everything here is maintained directly in Dataverse (Power Apps → Tables → Data), not in the canvas app.

  1. Calendars rts_calendar

    One row per calendar day — the backbone for every week and deadline calculation in the system. Must contain a complete, gap-free set of rows for every year in use, each with rts_date, rts_datetext (yyyyMMdd), rts_year, rts_weeknumber and rts_dayofweek.

    Set rts_holdiday + rts_holidayname for public holidays — deadlines falling on a holiday are shifted back to the previous working day.

  2. Suppliers rts_supplier1

    The rail / logistics service providers. Must have rts_suppliername, rts_suppliernameshort and rts_contactemail.

  3. Destinations rts_destination

    Receiving points (plants, compounds). Must have rts_destinationname and rts_destinationnameshort; add rts_vdscode and the address for the transport papers.

  4. Quelles / Factory Dispatch Points rts_factorydispatchpoint

    The physical origin of a transport. Must have rts_dispatchpointname and rts_factorydipatchnameshort. Set rts_reloadingroute = Yes for sites that hand over to a reloading point.

  5. Routes rts_route

    A lane = Factory Dispatch Point → Destination for one Supplier. Must have all three lookups plus rts_wagons (the standard quantity).

    rts_relativecriticalwagonamount is the critical share in percent. It decides, on loading confirmation, whether a transport ends at status 31 (loading complete) or 32 (critical quantity undershot, which opens dunning). Leave it empty and the split cannot be evaluated.

  6. Shareholders rts_shareholder

    Every person in the process. Must have rts_fullname, rts_email and at least one rts_role, plus the lookup that ties them to their organisation — rts_supplier, rts_destination or rts_factorydispatchpoint. BMW staff additionally get rts_user so the app can recognise them at sign-in.

  7. Deadlines rts_deadlines

    One row per transport status: for each weekday of the transport, which weekday the deadline falls on (rts_deadlinedaymonday … rts_deadlinedaysunday, with rts_deadlinedaydefault as fallback). Deadlines are computed in the week before the transport week.

    The matching rts_deadlinetime… fields are stored but not currently applied — the system works to deadline dates, not times.

  8. Reference Weeks rts_referenceweek Musterwoche

    The standing template: per rts_route and rts_weekday, how many wagons of which rts_wagonprofil (G1 / G2) and rts_wagontype (offen / geschlossen). This is what the week generator reads — a route with no reference-week row will not appear when a week is created.

    Where one destination is served by several suppliers, add one reference-week row per route.

  9. Reasons for Dunning Notices rts_reasonfordunningnotice

    The catalogue of short-delivery / non-performance reasons, grouped by rts_kategorie (BMW Intern, Dienstleister, Hafen/Compound, Witterung/Höhere Gewalt, Sonstiges/Unklar, Kein Grund angegeben). Must keep the leading numeric code in rts_dropdown (e.g. 07 Ausrangierung …) — the masks submit that code and the write-back flows resolve the reason record from it.

  10. Translations rts_translation

    All UI strings of the canvas app: rts_textid + rts_abbreviation (de / en) → rts_text. To change wording or add a language, add rows here — no app change is needed. A missing row shows as a blank label.

✕Never delete master data — deactivate it. Every table carries statecode (0 = Aktiv, 1 = Inaktiv). Historical transports keep pointing at the row; deleting it breaks those records and every report built on them. Set the row to Inaktiv and it disappears from the app's pick lists while history stays intact.
!Names are join keys. The notification flows do not follow the Factory Dispatch Point lookup — a transport carries the departure point as text (rts_calculateddeparturename), and the flows look the site up by rts_dispatchpointname or rts_factorydipatchnameshort. Rename a dispatch point in one place only and its notifications stop being delivered, with no error anywhere.

Running the system

Weekly rhythm: the Center of Competence generates the coming weeks from the Musterwoche, and from then on the flows move each transport along its status chain on a fixed schedule. Staff act only where a decision is required — checking a demand, checking an order, deciding on dunning or cancellation.

?When an expected e-mail does not arrive, check in this order:
  • Is the flow On? Solutions → UC_otd_3104 → Cloud flows.
  • Does the transport really match the filter — right status and right date? A transport already moved on by an earlier run is no longer picked up.

Connections

The flows run under connection references, not under the person who happens to open them: 3104_con_Microsoft Dataverse, 3104_con_Mail (all outgoing notifications), plus Office 365 Outlook and Microsoft Teams. At handover each of these must be re-pointed at an account the customer controls — ideally a service account, so a leaver does not stop the process.

!One technical rule to keep in mind: a flow that is called by another flow (today: F90, called by F22.R) must have all its connections set to embedded, not invoker. Switching one back to invoker breaks the call at runtime with a misleading error.

Open items at handover

!Points the customer should decide on or close.
  • App link in e-mails — 3104_env_AppUrl still points at the maker portal, so the RTS öffnen button does not open the app. Setting it to the app's player URL fixes every notification at once.
  • Mask hosting — the four partner masks are served from GitHub Pages under a private third-party account. They should be moved to customer-owned hosting before that account becomes unavailable.
  • Flow ownership — the flows created most recently are not yet shared with the RTS maker group; without that, only their creator can edit them.
  • Management screen — the KPI dashboard (screen 04) is laid out but not connected to data. It is the natural next development step.
  • Deadline times — rts_deadlinetime… is maintained but not evaluated; the process currently works to deadline dates only.
  • Transports on public holidays — these are still created, only the deadline is shifted. If they should be skipped or moved instead, that is a rule change to agree on.
